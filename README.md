# Tend

An AI-first, zero-admin household operating system for iPhone and iPad (iOS 26+).
Tend captures messy reality by voice, camera and shared documents, shows you at most three things that matter right
now, and helps you get them done in short, calm bursts. All routine AI runs on your device.
