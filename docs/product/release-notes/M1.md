# Release notes — M1 Core Engine & Cognitive Shielding

*Internal milestone. Nothing ships to the App Store in M1, so there is no "What's New" text yet.*
*Requirements: FR-B1, FR-B2, FR-B3 (data), UX-2, UX-3, P-1, P-2 · ACs: AC-M1-01…25*

## In one sentence

Tend now has a calm, private "brain" that picks at most three things that fit your moment, and quietly finds a new
time for anything that did not happen, without ever keeping score.

## What's new

- **At most three things.** The engine only ever hands out three tasks or fewer, chosen for your energy, the time of
  day, the day of the week and the weather. See [Why Tend shows you at most three things](../explanation/rule-of-three.md).
- **Gentle rescheduling.** When a task's time passes, a deep clean becomes a lighter version, an important task comes
  back gently, and everything else moves to its next good time. See
  [Why nothing in Tend ever counts against you](../explanation/compassionate-rescheduling.md).
- **Safe on your device.** Your household is saved locally and reloads exactly as it was. A damaged file is never
  replaced by an empty one.

## Known limitations

- There is no app or screen yet. The Now screen, Room Pulse and the 15-Minute Reset arrive in M3.
- There is no AI yet. Brain-dump capture arrives in M2.
- Tend does not fetch weather yet. It is planned for M3/M4, with coarse location only.
